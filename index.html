1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Anapra ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Bubble Burst Mania mobile game ("the Game").

We respect your privacy. Bubble Burst Mania is designed to be played without any account, sign-up, or login. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users.

In short: No registration. No personal data collected. Your game progress stays on your device. The only third-party service used is Google AdMob to display ads that keep the game free to play.

2. No Account Required

Bubble Burst Mania does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number.

All game data — such as your level progress, stars, coins, and settings — is stored locally on your device using standard storage. We have no access to this data and it is never transmitted to us.

3. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The Game does not ask for, store, or transmit your name, email, contacts, photos, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

However, our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may automatically collect limited non-personal technical data to serve ads. This is described in the next section.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bubble Burst Mania displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect certain information to deliver relevant ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. This may include:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android / IDFA on iOS)
  • Device type, model, operating system, and language
  • IP address (used for approximate region)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Diagnostic data for crash and error reporting

Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads

You can control whether you receive personalized ads through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
We never receive your personal identity from AdMob. AdMob operates independently under Google's own privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Bubble Burst Mania is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your region).

Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no personal data at risk from us. Any data collected by Google AdMob is secured by Google under their own strict security practices.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to delete or provide. However, you still have the following controls:

  • Opt out of personalized ads via your device settings (see Section 4).
  • Reset your advertising ID at any time from your device settings.
  • Uninstall the Game to remove all locally stored game data from your device.
