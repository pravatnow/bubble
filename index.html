1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Anapra ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Bubble Burst Dunia mobile game ("the Game").

We respect your privacy. Bubble Burst Dunia is designed to be played without an account, sign-up, or login. Game progress is stored locally on your device. The game also uses Google AdMob, whose software development kit (SDK) automatically collects and shares certain information from your device for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

In short: No registration is required. Your game progress stays on your device. Google AdMob processes certain device information, app interactions, diagnostics, and identifiers to provide and measure ads and help prevent fraud.

2. No Account Required

Bubble Burst Dunia does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number.

All game data — such as your level progress, stars, coins, and settings — is stored locally on your device using standard storage. We have no access to this data and it is never transmitted to us.

3. Information We Collect

Anapra does not ask players to provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, or photos, and the game does not require an account. Game progress, including levels, stars, coins, and settings, is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to Anapra.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK used by Google AdMob automatically collects and shares certain information from the device. Google identifies these data types as IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. Details and purposes are explained below.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bubble Burst Dunia displays advertisements through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google's Google Play data disclosure for the Android SDK, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following data types for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • IP address: may be used to estimate the general location of the device.
  • User product interactions: app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic information: technical information about app and SDK performance, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
  • Device and account identifiers: Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Data handling can vary by SDK version, settings, and optional features.

Advertising choices

Android users can review the advertising and privacy controls available on their device, including controls for the Android Advertising ID and ad personalization. The names and locations of these controls can vary by Android version and device manufacturer. Changing ad personalization preferences does not necessarily stop all data processing needed to deliver, measure, and protect advertising.

Anapra does not receive a player's name or email address through the game's own account system because the game has no account system. Google AdMob processes SDK data under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies. For details, review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

6. Children's Privacy

Bubble Burst Dunia is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your region). The game uses Google AdMob. Advertising SDK configuration and ad content must be appropriate for the app's actual target audience and comply with applicable Google Play Families and advertising requirements.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Anapra, please contact us using the details below.

7. Data Security

Game progress is stored locally on the device and Anapra does not operate a game account or game-progress server. Information collected and transmitted by Google AdMob is handled by Google under its own security and privacy practices. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Rights

Anapra does not maintain player accounts or receive locally stored game progress. You can remove local game data by uninstalling the Game (or by clearing its storage through Android settings). You can also review the advertising ID and ad-personalization controls provided by Android. For requests concerning information processed by Google, please review Google's privacy resources and account controls.
